## Asset acceptance gates

- Obtain the missing ordered archive/manifest before claiming 165 supplied image references are locally available.

- Separate evidence screenshots, product photography, logo assets and generated derivatives.
- Every public/admin media record needs a named owner, alt text, visibility state, storage path, and audit trail.

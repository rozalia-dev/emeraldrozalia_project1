## Status vocabulary

- **Verified:** direct execution and required evidence are attached to the same commit.
- **Partial:** a related route/model/view/test foundation exists, but one or more required behavior, authorization, synchronization, responsive, accessibility, visual, or release gates remain open.
- **Unverified:** the source or behavior has not been objectively demonstrated.
- **Not found:** the targeted route/consumer/workflow was not found.
- **Partial · explicit gap:** a concrete route, synchronization, or fixture defect is recorded in the evidence cell.

The status column below is intentionally conservative. Every row remains open for the later visual, browser, accessibility, contract, and release gates.
